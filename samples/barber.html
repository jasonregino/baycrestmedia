Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is open. Booking online holds your spot and your barber, which we recommend on weekends.

How do I book a specific barber?

Book online and pick your barber and time, about thirty seconds. Or give us a call.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

We do. Kids twelve and under have their own rate, and our barbers are great with first-timers.

Is there parking?

Street parking on 4th Avenue and the city garage a block over.
