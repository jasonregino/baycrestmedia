Good to Know

Frequently asked

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients are welcome, and the new-patient exam, cleaning, and X-rays are $99. Book online or give us a call.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most PPO plans and file the claim for you. If you're not sure, send us your plan and we'll check before your visit.

I get nervous at the dentist.

You're in good company, and we do this gently. Tell us at check-in and we'll go at your pace. Sedation options are available for longer treatment.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes, we keep same-day slots open for emergencies when we can. Call us first thing and we'll get you in.
